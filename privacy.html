Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

video-factory is used by one person, its owner, with his own Google account.

  • It uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos, captions and thumbnails to his own channels, and to read back the settings of those uploads.
  • It accesses only the channels the owner signs in to. It reads no other person's data.
  • The sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to anyone except Google.
  • It collects no personal data from visitors or viewers, uses no cookies or analytics of its own, and shares nothing with third parties.

Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page; deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes the stored sign-in.
